[h2] About the NFL Slate
[p] The week laid out game by game with the defensive read attached. Where the other NFL boards rank all 32 defenses against each other, this answers the game-shaped question: in this matchup, what does this defense give up?
[h3] How to read it
[p] Each game carries the two defenses with what they concede through the air and on the ground, alongside the pass catchers whose usage lines up with those weaknesses. A soft spot only matters if someone on the other side works that area, so the player list beside each defense is the part that turns a rank into a matchup.
