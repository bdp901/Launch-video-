About the NFL Slate

▶

The week laid out game by game with the defensive read attached. Where the other NFL boards rank all 32 defenses against each other, this answers the game-shaped question: in this matchup, what does this defense give up?

How to read it

Each game carries the two defenses with what they concede through the air and on the ground, alongside the pass catchers whose usage lines up with those weaknesses. A soft spot only matters if someone on the other side works that area, so the player list beside each defense is the part that turns a rank into a matchup.
